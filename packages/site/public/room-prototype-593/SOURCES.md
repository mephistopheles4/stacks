Everything else in each room (wall, floor, rug, table, candles, lamp) is built by
`build_room.py`.

With `--lamp hero`, the standing lamp is our own design, built by `hero_lamp.py` (made
here): a barley-twist bronze column, a scrolled bracket and a fluted amber shade. Its
general style was inspired by "Standing Lamp" by berylvanness on Sketchfab
(<https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/1adfc9dce86b4ea9aaf138b1e0e6f343>, CC BY 4.0); none
of that model's geometry, textures or files were used.

With `--lightmaps`, the floor, the rug and its fringe bake light only (`light/<room>-*.webp`,
made here), and the page lays these textures over it. Every baked atlas ships as `bake/<room>-*.webp` (made here), beside the GLB rather than packed in it: the site's CSP refuses the blob: URL a packed image is read through. `tex/rug-fringe.webp` (the fringe's
cotton) and `tex/rug-pile.webp` (the pile grain) are generated by `rug.py`, made here.

## `room-audition.glb`: the furnished room

`room-hero.glb`'s room, furnished by `build_room.py --dress dress/audition_dress.py` from the
owner's block-out. The dress scripts in `room-prototype-593/dress/` place, tint and group what
is listed here (made here); the throw over the chest, the frames' mouldings and the side walls
are built by them. Every piece is baked with the room's light: `bake/room-audition-*.webp`, and
for a split group its colour in `bake/` and its light in `light/` (made here).

The rug in this room is the Met's carpet above, 3.49 × 2.57 m here (`--rug-span`), with a
fold where the desk chair's back feet stand on its end (`--rug-ruck`), shoved up by a cloth
simulation (`rug.py`).

⚠️ **`room-trellis.glb` must not ship as it is, and is not committed**: it stays on
the machine that made it. The Met photo had no alpha
channel, so the Space cut the chair out with `briaai/BRIA-RMBG-2.0`, which is
licensed for non-commercial use only. The Space skips that step for an image that
already has an alpha channel, so a shippable chair means masking the photo first
(BiRefNet, MIT, or by hand) and uploading that PNG.
